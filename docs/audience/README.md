# Living Water System | Audience Guide

Welcome. The **Living Water System** is a freely shared, open-source conceptual engineering research project exploring water lifting, hydraulic air compression, conveyance, distributed treatment, and infrastructure resilience.

Different readers will approach the same work from different directions. This folder will provide short, audience-specific introductions that point back to the shared engineering record.

## Find your starting point

- **Communities and citizens:** Why resilient water access matters, what the concepts propose, and what participation might look like.
- **Engineers and researchers:** Mechanisms, assumptions, unresolved questions, calculations to develop, and physical tests to run.
- **Water utilities and operators:** Opportunities for hybrid operation, maintainability, asset management, and integration with existing systems.
- **Governments and institutions:** Public outcomes, resilience, governance, and ways to evaluate pilot proposals.
- **Universities and students:** Research questions, experiments, competing interpretations, and opportunities to contribute findings.

These audience introductions are **planned**; this guide is the starting page.

## A shared foundation

This project is an **idea gift**. Readers are invited to examine, test, improve, adapt, or reject its individual mechanisms on engineering grounds. If you develop something useful, sharing your findings, calculations, drawings, or improvements back through GitHub is appreciated but voluntary.

**NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION.** The materials are conceptual and preliminary, not final calculations, drawings, or designs. Anyone adopting them is responsible for independently completing and verifying the engineering and obtaining required approvals.
